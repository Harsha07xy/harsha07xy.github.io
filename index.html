Why Play Games?

Gaming Is Good for You.

Puzzle games aren't just fun — they sharpen your mind, reduce stress, and boost creativity. Here's why you should play every day.

Boosts Brain Power

Puzzle games improve memory, logical thinking, and problem-solving skills. Studies show that just 15–30 minutes of daily puzzle gameplay can enhance cognitive function and keep your brain sharp at any age.

Reduces Stress & Anxiety

Playing casual puzzle games triggers a flow state — your mind focuses on the game and forgets worries. Research shows that mobile gaming for 20–30 minutes daily can significantly lower cortisol (stress hormone) levels.

Improves Focus & Attention

Games like One Stroke and Letter Hunt require sustained concentration. Regular play trains your attention span and helps you focus better at work, studies, and everyday tasks. Even 10 minutes a day makes a difference.

Sparks Creativity

Puzzle games push you to think outside the box. When you approach a tricky level from different angles, you're training your brain to find creative solutions — a skill that transfers to real life challenges.

Fun for All Ages

Whether you're a student, a working professional, or a senior — puzzle games are universally enjoyable. They're easy to learn, require no special skills, and provide a healthy break from screen fatigue.

Boosts Mood & Happiness

Completing a challenging puzzle level releases dopamine — the "feel-good" chemical in your brain. This instant sense of accomplishment keeps you motivated, happy, and coming back for more!

How Long Should You Play?

Experts recommend 20–40 minutes of puzzle gaming per day for the best cognitive benefits. Short, focused sessions are more effective than long marathons. Take breaks every 20 minutes to rest your eyes. A few focused rounds of One Stroke or Letter Hunt during your morning commute, lunch break, or before bed is the perfect balance of fun and brain training!
