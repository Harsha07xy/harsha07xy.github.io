Runner.io: Jump & Dodge – Data Deletion Request

App: Runner.io: Jump & Dodge
Developer: VOIDRISE

If you would like your data from this game to be deleted, please follow the steps below.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to voidrise.games@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my data: Runner.io
  3. In the email, include the player name / ID shown in the game (for example on the leaderboard), so we can find your entry.
  4. We will delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Send deletion request by email

What data is deleted

  • Your leaderboard entry (player name / ID and scores).
  • Any other player data linked to your player ID that is stored on our servers.

What data is not deleted or kept

  • Data stored only on your own device (game progress, coins, settings) is not on our servers. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear the app's data in your phone settings.
  • Advertising data collected by Google AdMob is controlled by Google, not by us. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your phone's Settings (Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID).

Retention period

After a deletion request is processed, your data is removed from our systems. Any backup copies are removed within 30 days.

Questions? Contact us at voidrise.games@gmail.com.
